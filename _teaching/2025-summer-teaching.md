During my postdoctoral appointment at IFW Dresden, I served as a tutor for this course, holding weekly tutorials and grading exams. The course covered topics such as the limitations of classical physics, old quantum theory, wavefunctions, Schrödinger’s equation and its solution in various one-dimensional potentials, the simple harmonic oscillator, quantum mechanics in three dimensions, the hydrogen atom, spin-1/2, addition of angular momenta, perturbation theory.
