During my postdoctoral appointment at IFW Dresden, I served as a tutor for this course offered from April to July 2025, where I conducted weekly tutorials and graded exams. The course covered topics such as the limitations of classical physics, old quantum theory, wavefunctions, Schrödinger’s equation and its solution in various one-dimensional potentials, the simple harmonic oscillator, quantum mechanics in three dimensions, the hydrogen atom, spin-1/2, addition of angular momenta, and perturbation theory.
